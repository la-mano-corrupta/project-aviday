# Philosopher Playbook, Topic Matrix & Quote Verification Ledger

## 1. Strategic Positioning: Depth Without Clickbait

The short-form philosophy market splits into two lanes:
1. **Disposable "Dark Psychology / Alpha" Slop:** Repetitive dark B-roll, misattributed quotes, aggressive commands ("erase everyone"), shallow Machiavellianism. High churn, low trust, demonetization risk ("inauthentic content").
2. **Humane Animated Philosophical Storytelling:** Models like *Sisyphus 55*, *Pursuit of Wonder*, *Einzelgänger*, *The School of Life*, *Academy of Ideas*, and *Philosophy Vibe*—connecting timeless thinkers to modern anxiety, conformity, burnout, self-deception, and meaning.

Always choose lane 2. Ground every Short in a real psychological tension, a verifiable thinker or historical moment, and an honest takeaway.

### B. Secondary / Historical Reports (Attribute as Reported, Not Verbatim Speech)

- **Socrates — "I know that I know nothing":** Not spoken verbatim as a standalone epigram in Plato; paraphrased from Plato's *Apology* 21d and reported in **Diogenes Laërtius** (*Lives of Eminent Philosophers* II.32). Say *"Plato describes Socrates realizing..."* rather than putting it inside quote marks as a slogan.
- **Diogenes of Sinope — "Stand a little out of my sunshine" & "I am looking for an honest human":** Diogenes left zero surviving writings. Both anecdotes come from secondary ancient biographers (**Plutarch**, *Life of Alexander* 14; **Diogenes Laërtius**, *Lives* VI.38 & VI.41). Frame accurately: *"Ancient historians record that when Alexander asked..."*
