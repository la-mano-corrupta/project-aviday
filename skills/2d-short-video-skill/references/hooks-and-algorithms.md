# Platform Algorithms & Short-Form Hook Engineering

## 1. Algorithmic Physics (2025–2026 Platform Signals)

### YouTube Shorts
- **March 31, 2025 View-Count Shift:** YouTube began counting every Short playback start and replay loop as a "View" with zero minimum watch threshold (extended across formats by August 24, 2026). **Never judge performance by vanity raw views.**
- **True Ranking & Monetization Signals:**
  1. **Viewed vs. Swiped Away** (goal: **>= 70–75%** viewed; anything below 60% signals a weak 0–3s hook).
  2. **Engaged Views** (the metric governing monetization and distribution expansion).
  3. **Average Percentage Viewed / Completion & Loop Rewatch** (videos in the **15–45s** pocket consistently achieve **95–115%+** retention when ended with a seamless loop or dense reveal).
- **Volume Compounding:** Creator analytics show steady momentum compounds noticeably once channels cross ~200 consistent Shorts, iterating hooks via the "exploit" testing loop.

### Instagram Reels
- **Mosseri's Core Signals (2025–2026):**
  1. **Watch Time** (survival past the **3-second threshold**—target **50%+** survival—before IG expands beyond initial test cohorts).
  2. **Sends Per Reach (DM Shares):** The single strongest signal for non-follower distribution (industry benchmarks estimate **3× to 5×** the weight of likes).
  3. **Saves & Likes Per Reach:** Saves carry roughly **~3×** the weight of likes for evergreen philosophical/psychological explainers.
- **Late-2025 IG Structural Rules:**
  - **5-Hashtag Cap:** Instagram limits captions to **5 hashtags**; use 3–5 hyper-specific topic tags (`#philosophy`, `#stoicism`, `#absurdism`, `#carl_jung`, `#2danimation`) rather than generic spam (`#fyp`, `#viral`).
  - **"Your Algorithm" Topical Panel:** Users can manage Reels topics directly—consistent philosophical framing helps Instagram cluster your account into the right intellectual/self-reflection audience.
  - **Trial Reels:** Test 2–3 competing 0–3s hook cuts on non-followers before locking a series template.

### TikTok
- Focuses on **3-second hold**, **full completion**, **rewatches**, **comments/debates**, and **shares**.
- Notice safe-zone difference: TikTok overlays are narrower (~480 px bottom, ~140 px right) than YouTube Shorts (672 px bottom, 192 px right). Always author to the composite **All-3** zone so one render works everywhere.

## 2. Optimal Runtimes & Spoken Word Ceilings

Conversational philosophy narration runs at **~150 WPM (2.5 words/sec)**. Subtract **10–15% pause margin** so dramatic `[beat]` and `[pause]` breaths actually land:

| Format | Target Duration | Raw Ceiling (2.5 wps) | Recommended Spoken Target | Scene Cadence | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micro-Loop** | **15 s** | ~38 words | **30–35 words** | 2–3 crisp cuts (1.5–2.5s visual shifts) | Quote myth-busting, single paradox, seamless replay loop |
| **Discovery Short** | **30 s** | 75 words | **65–70 words** (75 max) | 4–5 beats (~6s each; sub-shifts every 2–3s) | Diogenes anecdotes, *One Metaphor* concepts, Reels non-follower velocity |

| **Deep Contrast** | **60 s** | 150 words | **125–140 words** (150 max) | 7–9 scenes + second-15 pattern interrupt | Head-to-head contrast (Kant vs. Mill, Camus vs. Sartre) |

## 3. The 0–3 Second Multimodal Hook System

Mobile feeds run on thumb-muscle autopilot. Frame 1 has **zero greetings**, **zero channel logos**, and **zero slow fade-ins**. Synchronize **all three layers simultaneously**:

1. **Spoken Audio Hook (7–8 words max):** Launch mid-tension in the opening 3 seconds. Focus on a *cost*, a *paradox*, a *misconception*, or an *unbelievable historical scene* (leveraging negative bias + the Zeigarnik open-loop effect).
2. **On-Screen Text Hook (5–8 words max):** Bold, one-glance headline inside the upper-middle All-3 safe box (`y = 380..620`, centered at `x = 468`) so muted scrollers grasp the stakes in <1 second.
3. **Frame-1 Visual Motion:** Immediate 2D character close-up, high-contrast metaphor prop (glowing boulder, ceramic jar, cracked mask), or snap zoom—never a blank title card.

## 4. Six Proven Philosophy Hook Archetypes

### Archetype 1: Misconception Break (High Viewed-vs-Swiped)
- **Psychology:** Shatters a familiar belief ("everything you heard about X is wrong"), triggering instant cognitive dissonance.
- **Template:** `[Thinker/Quote] never meant [popular cliché]—it was [dark/shocking truth].`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Socrates never actually said that famous quote."* | Text: `SOCRATES NEVER SAID THIS`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Nietzsche wasn't celebrating when he wrote God's dead."* | Text: `NIETZSCHE'S REAL WARNING`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Stoicism was never about hiding your feelings."* | Text: `THE BIGGEST STOIC LIE`

### Archetype 2: Historical Rebel / Shock Anecdote (High Rewatch & Follows)
- **Psychology:** Drops the viewer straight into a cinematic confrontation between status and wisdom.
- **Template:** `[Powerful figure] offered [thinker] anything. He asked for [absurdly small/defiant thing].`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Alexander the Great offered him half the world."* | Text: `HE TOLD AN EMPEROR: MOVE`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"He carried a lit lantern in broad daylight."* | Text: `WHY DIOGENES HELD A LAMP`

### Archetype 3: Second-Person Existential Callout (High DM *Sends Per Reach*)
- **Psychology:** Names an unspoken private habit or exhaustion so accurately the viewer feels seen and texts it to a friend ("this is literally us").
- **Template:** `Ever notice why you [relatable modern habit] when nobody's watching?`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"You rehearse fake arguments in the shower because..."* | Text: `SARTRE EXPLAINED YOUR BRAIN`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"You don't actually hate Mondays. You hate this."* | Text: `CAMUS ON YOUR ROUTINE`

### Archetype 4: Thought-Experiment Setup (High Comment Debate)
- **Psychology:** Forces the viewer into an immediate moral or existential choice inside 3 seconds.
- **Template:** `Imagine a machine that gives you [impossible promise]—would you step in?`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"One pill deletes every regret—would you swallow it?"* | Text: `THE EXPERIENCE MACHINE`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"You wake up chained facing a shadow wall."* | Text: `PLATO'S CAVE IN 30 SECONDS`

### Archetype 5: Paradox / Psychological Reframe (High Saves)
- **Psychology:** Links two opposite ideas (*wanting calm causes anxiety*, *closer connection causes pain*).
- **Template:** `The harder you chase [desired feeling], the faster it [backfires].`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Chasing happiness is the fastest way to suffer."* | Text: `ALAN WATTS' BACKWARDS LAW`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Getting close to people feels like freezing porcupines."* | Text: `SCHOPENHAUER'S HEDGEHOG DILEMMA`

### Archetype 6: Direct Warning / Silent Cost (Negative Bias 3× Hold)
- **Psychology:** Activates survival loss-aversion before the thumb can swipe.
- **Template:** `If you keep [common social habit], you are slowly [hidden philosophical cost].`
- **Examples:**
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Living for strangers' applause quietly erases your life."* | Text: `MARCUS AURELIUS WARNED YOU`
  - Spoken (8 words): *"Scrolling to escape boredom destroys your mind's depth."* | Text: `PASCAL'S ONE-ROOM TEST`

## 5. Mid-Video Retention & Loop-Ready Endings

### Preventing the Second-15 Drop-Off
A strong 0–3s hook followed by flat exposition causes a "delayed swipe" around seconds 10–18. Prevent it with:
- **Visual Reset Every 1.5–2.5s (Canvas/Remotion):** Camera scale nudge, background polarity flip (cream -> charcoal), prop transformation, or 2-frame impact shake.
- **The Second-12–15 Socratic Interrupt (*Philosophy Vibe* device):** Let a Skeptic character pop in with the exact objection forming in the viewer's mind:
  - *"Wait—if nothing matters, why even get out of bed?"* -> Narrator snaps back with Camus' revolt.
- **Open Loops:** Delay the named philosopher or punchline turn until seconds 18–22 while escalating the metaphor visually.

### Seamless Loop & Share-Trigger Endings
Never end with *"Like and subscribe for part 2"*—it collapses final-second completion and triggers immediate swipe-away.
- **Audio/Syntax Seamless Loop:** Write the last line so its final preposition or clause reads straight into Spoken Hook line 1:
  - *End:* `"...which is exactly why..."` -> *Loops into Hook:* `"...chasing happiness is the fastest way to suffer."`
- **DM-Share Stinger:** Close with a crisp, memorable mirror line (6–9 words) that triggers a screenshot or DM share (*"Stop renting out your peace to strangers."*).

## 6. Post-Upload Diagnostic Rubric

Inspect YouTube Studio / IG Insights after 24–48 hours:
- **Drop at 0–3s (`Viewed < 65%`):** Spoken hook >8 words, slow start, weak muted Frame-1 contrast, or text hook buried in unsafe margins.
- **Drop at 10–18s:** Exposition bogged down, too many abstractions without a concrete 2D metaphor, or no visual reset for >3 seconds.
- **Drop at last 3s:** End card/CTA telegraphed the ending too early. Cut trailing silence and tighten into a loop or sting.
