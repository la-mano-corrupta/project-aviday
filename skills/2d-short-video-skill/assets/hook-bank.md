# Philosophy 2D Hook Bank (0–3s Multimodal Templates)

Every hook specifies three synchronized layers: **Spoken (<=8 words)** · **Text (<=8 words)** · **Frame-1 Visual Motion**.

---

## 1. Misconception Break (Beats Quote Slop)
- **Template:** `[Thinker] never meant [cliché]—he warned about [truth].`
  - Spoken: `"Nietzsche wasn't celebrating when he wrote God's dead."` (8w) · Text: `NIETZSCHE'S REAL WARNING` · Visual: Candle snaps out on dark parchment; distressed shadow silhouette.
  - Spoken: `"Socrates never actually said that famous wisdom quote."` (8w) · Text: `SOCRATES NEVER SAID THIS` · Visual: Red ink X slams across viral quote scroll.
  - Spoken: `"Stoicism was never about suppressing your emotions."` (7w) · Text: `THE BIGGEST STOIC MYTH` · Visual: Stone bust cracks open to show calm glowing embers inside.

## 2. Historical Rebel / Anecdote (Rewatches + Follows)
- **Template:** `[Ruler/Crowd] offered [Thinker] everything; he chose [shock].`
  - Spoken: `"The world's richest emperor offered him literally anything."` (8w) · Text: `OFFERED ANYTHING` · Visual: Towering golden crown shadow falls over tiny clay jar.
  - Spoken: `"Socrates chose deadly poison over running away."` (7w) · Text: `WHY SOCRATES STAYED` · Visual: Open prison door vs. glowing cup centered in spotlight.
  - Spoken: `"Seven days before dying, he wrote twenty-one rules."` (8w) · Text: `MUSASHI'S FINAL RULES` · Visual: Katana brush writes crisp Japanese ink circle (*enso*) on Frame 1.

## 3. Second-Person Existential Callout (DM *Sends Per Reach*)
- **Template:** `You don't hate [surface symptom]; you hate [hidden philosophical root].`
  - Spoken: `"You don't hate Mondays; you hate feeling replaceable."` (8w) · Text: `CAMUS ON YOUR JOB` · Visual: Line of identical paper silhouettes walking onto a spinning conveyor.
  - Spoken: `"You rehearse fake arguments because you fear being seen."` (9w -> tighten: `"You rehearse fake arguments in the shower."` 7w) · Text: `SARTRE'S "THE LOOK"` · Visual: Mirror glass fractures into watching eyes.
  - Spoken: `"Scrolling every quiet minute is slowly erasing your voice."` (9w -> tighten: `"Never sitting alone quietly is ruining your mind."` 8w) · Text: `PASCAL'S ONE-ROOM TEST` · Visual: Person alone in bare box reaches frantically for glowing phone.

## 4. Thought-Experiment Setup (Comment Debates)
- **Template:** `Imagine [impossible premise]—would you [irreversible choice]?`
  - Spoken: `"One machine simulates a perfect life—would you plug in?"` (9w -> tighten: `"A machine guarantees total happiness. Plug in?"` 7w) · Text: `NOZICK'S EXPERIENCE MACHINE` · Visual: Glowing neural wire hovering inches from smiling sleeping character.
  - Spoken: `"You replace every wooden plank on your ship."` (8w) · Text: `IS IT STILL YOURS?` · Visual: 2D wooden ship swaps planks one by one in rapid frame pop.
  - Spoken: `"You design society without knowing who you'll be."` (8w) · Text: `RAWLS' VEIL OF IGNORANCE` · Visual: Blindfolded figure draws cards above a miniature city grid.

## 5. Paradox / Psychological Reframe (High Saves)
- **Template:** `Chasing [goal] is the exact reason you feel [opposite].`
  - Spoken: `"Chasing happiness is the fastest way to suffer."` (8w) · Text: `THE BACKWARDS LAW` · Visual: Character runs on tilted wheel chasing dangling sun that stays fixed.
  - Spoken: `"Getting closer to people hurts like freezing porcupines."` (8w) · Text: `THE HEDGEHOG DILEMMA` · Visual: Two spiky 2D silhouettes shiver, step together, flinch apart.
  - Spoken: `"Every distraction you buy quietly steals your time."` (8w) · Text: `SENECA ON TIME` · Visual: Hourglass sand turns into tiny gold coins slipping through fingers.

## 6. Direct Warning / Silent Cost (Negative Bias Hold)
- **Template:** `Stop letting [external force] price your [internal treasure].`
  - Spoken: `"Stop renting out your peace to noisy strangers."` (8w) · Text: `MARCUS AURELIUS ON PEACE` · Visual: Keyhole in head with noisy speech bubbles pouring inside.
  - Spoken: `"A tree survives the axe by looking useless."` (8w) · Text: `ZHUANGZI'S USELESS TREE` · Visual: Straight trees chopped down; gnarled crooked tree stands untouched in sunlight.
